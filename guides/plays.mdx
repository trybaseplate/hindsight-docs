**Plays** turns the signals in your Deal Reviews into proactive, account-specific guidance for reps. Define the deal conditions that matter, then Hindsight identifies matching opportunities and delivers the next best action, grounded in the deal's evidence.

Use Plays throughout the revenue lifecycle: help a rep prepare for a competitor entering an in-progress opportunity, address a new risk before a key meeting, guide an expansion motion, or re-engage a deal that went cold. Win-backs are one useful play type—not the only one.

Plays sit on top of your [Deal Reviews](/deals-quickstart). The verified record, CRM data, deal evidence, and scored decision drivers Hindsight has assembled for each opportunity make guidance specific to the account—not a generic sequence or static alert.

## What a Play is

A Play is a reusable, proactive guidance motion defined by two things:

- **Enrollment criteria** — filters over your deals and their Deal Review fields. A matching deal can enroll automatically when its insights refresh, or you can enroll selected deals manually. For example:

  > **Auto-enroll deals where**
  > a competitor is present **and** the deal is in evaluation **and** product fit is below the team's target

- **Play guidance** — instructions for the action Hindsight should recommend to the rep. For example: *"Review the competitive risk, cite the most relevant evidence, and prepare a call plan for the account owner."*

You can also attach playbooks, messaging decks, battlecards, and other Asset Library documents. Hindsight uses them as reference material when it creates the guidance.

Because enrollment runs from the deal record, each play is a live list of opportunities where your team can act—not a static report.

## Set up a Play

<Steps>
  <Step icon="target" title="Define the criteria">
    Use deal filters to describe the moment this play should address. Combine CRM fields with Hindsight's Deal Review signals—such as stage, competitor, product fit, customer fit, and decision drivers—to target in-progress deals as well as closed-won, closed-lost, or expansion opportunities. For example: *deals in late-stage evaluation with a newly identified competitor*, *accounts ready for expansion*, or *high-fit opportunities lost to sales execution*.
  </Step>

  <Step icon="wand-magic-sparkles" title="Let Hindsight generate the play">
    Write clear Play guidance that tells Hindsight the desired outcome, format, and action. For every enrolled opportunity, Paige researches the deal and produces a **recommended play** from its evidence, comparable signals, and your instructions. That could be a call plan, risk response, internal coaching brief, stakeholder strategy, or outreach—not just a win-back message.
  </Step>

  <Step icon="link" title="Attach assets">
    Link Asset Library documents that should inform the recommendation: battlecards, playbooks, one-pagers, messaging decks, or product updates. Their content is included when Paige drafts guidance for this play.
  </Step>

  <Step icon="slack" title="Act in Slack">
    Choose whether to notify the deal owner, additional teammates, or both. When an opportunity enrolls, the recipients receive the evidence-led Play in Slack, with options to ask Paige for help, open the associated Hindsight research session, or snooze it. Email notifications can also alert recipients to a new Play.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Track outcomes

Each play tracks the opportunities enrolled in it and their progress—from **pending** and **surfaced** to **engaged**, **snoozed**, **dismissed**, or **converted**. Use that view to see which proactive motions create engagement and where to refine your criteria or guidance.

# Pages internal launch kit

Prepared November 3, 2026. Draft copy for an internal preview; deployment and workspace availability have not been verified. Confirm access in the intended internal workspace before sending. Email recipients and Slack channel are intentionally unspecified.

## Email

Subject: Try Pages: turn Hindsight research into a saved brief or report

Hi team,

We're opening Pages for internal testing. It gives the HTML reports, deal briefs, and guides you create with Hindsight a shared home, so you can return to the work and share it with colleagues in your workspace.

Try it with something you're working on:

1. Open Chat and ask: “Create an HTML page summarizing why we lost competitive deals last quarter. Include supporting deal examples, the main objections, and recommended actions for sales.”
2. Open the page link, review the evidence, and find the saved output in Pages.
3. Share its link with a colleague in the same workspace or download the HTML file.

In Pages → Templates, you can preview layouts or upload an HTML template for your workspace. Supported skills can also have a preferred template.

Be sure to ask for an **HTML page**; a report or brief request alone still returns Markdown. Page links require Hindsight sign-in and access to the same workspace.

Please reply with your prompt, the page link, and what worked or fell short. We'd especially like feedback on evidence quality, layout, template reuse, and downloads.

Open Pages: https://app.usehindsight.com/pages

Thanks!

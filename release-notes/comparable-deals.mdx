## What it does

<New category="Deal Intelligence" />

For any deal, Comparable Deals builds a shortlist of your closest matches from closed business — weighing product, customer profile, competitors, and how recently each comparable deal closed — and scores each one for how relevant it actually is, not just how similar it looks on paper.

**What it means for you:**
- **See the pattern, not just the deal.** Instead of relying on memory or a single anecdote, pull up the handful of past deals that actually predict how this one will go.
- **Evidence, not a black box.** Each comparable deal comes with an audit of the evidence behind its score, so you can see why Hindsight thinks it's relevant — and dismiss the ones that aren't.
- **Deeper research when it's needed.** When the evidence on hand isn't enough to score a candidate deal with confidence, Comparable Deals can call on Paige to research it further before finalizing the match.

## A playground for tuning the algorithm

<New category="Deal Intelligence" />

Admins get a playground to test and tune how comparable deals are matched and scored before rolling changes out to the team — adjust which dimensions matter most, preview results against real deals, and iterate before committing to a configuration.
